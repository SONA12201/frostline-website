# FrostLine Cold Chain Logistics (class project)

**Business:** Cold chain logistics company in Gujarat.
**Problem:** Customers lose stock because they cannot see whether cargo stayed in its temperature range.
**Customer:** Pharma distributors, dairies, frozen food makers.
**App:** Shipment tracker with live temperature plus a manager dashboard.
**Dashboard answers:** How many shipments are delayed, how many had temperature excursions, and is delivery on time?
